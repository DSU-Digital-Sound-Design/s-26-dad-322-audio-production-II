title: "Record modes"
---

### Punch In / Punch Out

Punching in and out lets you re-record a specific section of a track without affecting the rest.

#### Time Selection Auto Punch

Time Selection Auto Punch lets you define a section for re-recording so Reaper automatically punches in and out at the boundaries.

**Setting Time Selection:**

- Click and drag on the timeline to select the section you want to replace.

**Configuring Recording Mode:**

- Right-click the record button at the bottom of your toolbar.
- Select "Record mode: Time Selection Auto Punch".

**Setting Up Pre-roll:**

- In the metronome/pre-roll settings, activate **pre-roll before recording**. This plays back a few seconds before the time selection so the performer can get in sync.
- Pre-roll also makes it easier to edit between the new recording and the surrounding material.

#### Selected Item Auto-Punch

Selected Item Auto-Punch is useful when you have multiple sections across the track that need re-recording.

**Slicing and Selecting Items:**

- Use the split tool to slice the track at the beginning and end of each section you want to re-record.
- Select these individual items — Reaper will only record over the selected items.

**Recording:**

- Hit the record button. Reaper automatically punches in and out of the selected items.

**Comping and Finalizing:**

- After recording, comp the takes to choose the best parts of each recording.
