## Compressors

**Fairly Childish Compressor/Limiter** (Stillwell)
   - **Function**: A Fairchild 670-inspired variable-mu compressor/limiter with program-dependent release. The name is a pun on the vintage Fairchild 670 tube compressor.
   - **Application on Bus**: Provides warm, musical compression with a natural-sounding release, letting transients breathe while gluing elements together cohesively.

**Fairly Childish Stereo Compressor/Limiter** (Stillwell)
   - **Function**: Stereo version of the Fairly Childish, aimed at processing stereo signals.
   - **Application on Bus**: Ensures balanced compression across the stereo field, maintaining the spatial integrity of stereo recordings.

## Limiters

**Master Limiter**
   - **Function**: A mastering-grade limiter designed to maximize loudness while preventing clipping.
   - **Application on Bus**: Useful for achieving a louder mix without introducing distortion, ensuring the bus sits well in the overall mix.

**MGA JS Limiter**
   - **Function**: A peak limiter aimed at controlling transient spikes in audio signals.
   - **Application on Bus**: Effective in managing unexpected peaks, keeping dynamics in check without affecting the overall punch.

**MGA JS Limiter (Unlinked Stereo)**
   - **Function**: A variant of the MGA JS Limiter that processes left and right channels independently.
   - **Application on Bus**: Allows for independent limiting of each channel, useful when a stereo mix has uneven dynamics between the left and right channels.

**NP1136 Peak Limiter** (Liteon)
   - **Function**: A program-dependent peak limiter with tone-shaping controls and a mix-with-original option.
   - **Application on Bus**: Provides smooth peak limiting while preserving dynamic excitement; effective at gentle gain reductions of 3–8 dB.

**Simple Peak-1 Limiter**
   - **Function**: A basic peak limiter for controlling maximum signal levels.
   - **Application on Bus**: Offers straightforward peak control, suitable for quick fixes to prevent clipping.

**Turquoise Limiter S2** (Tukan)
   - **Function**: A limiter plugin designed for precise control over dynamic range.
   - **Application on Bus**: Offers detailed limiting options, allowing for fine-tuning of dynamics to achieve a polished and cohesive sound.

## Clippers

**Event Horizon Limiter/Clipper** (Stillwell)
   - **Function**: A transparent clipper with an optional soft-clip circuit. Uses clipping rather than gain reduction to control peaks, resulting in zero latency and zero pumping artifacts.
   - **Application on Bus**: Ideal for taming transients and achieving loudness increases without the pumping or frequency artifacts associated with limiting.

**Soft Clipper/Limiter**
   - **Function**: Combines soft clipping and limiting to manage peaks and add harmonic saturation.
   - **Application on Bus**: Adds warmth and subtle saturation, enhancing the overall tonal character while controlling peaks.
