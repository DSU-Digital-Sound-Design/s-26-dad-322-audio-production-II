## Transient Shapers

**[Flux BitterSweet](https://www.flux.audio/project/bittersweet-freeware/)** (Free)
   - **Function**: Single-knob transient designer. Turning toward *Bitter* magnifies transients; toward *Sweet* reduces them. Includes Fast/Medium/Slow processing modes and M/S channel processing options.
   - **Application**: Quick, intuitive transient control on drums, percussion, or any bus. Good starting point when you want to add or pull back attack without complex setup.

**[Auburn Sounds Couture](https://www.auburnsounds.com/products/Couture.html)** (Free)
   - **Function**: Threshold-independent transient shaper using a 2-band spectral RMS detector. Detects transients based on the rate of level change rather than a fixed threshold, making it effective on complex material like acoustic guitar and piano where not all transients exceed surrounding signal levels.
   - **Application**: More precise than a single-knob shaper. Use when BitterSweet is too blunt — for example, smoothing out plucky guitar attack or adding consistency to acoustic drum hits. The free version includes full transient shaping; the paid version adds saturation.

## Tempo-Driven Effects

**[TAL-Filter-2](https://tal-software.com/products/tal-filter)** (Free)
   - **Function**: Host-synced filter and volume modulation plugin. Supports multiple filter types, LFO-driven volume modulation (trance gate), and stereo panorama modulation, all locked to DAW tempo via MIDI NoteOn trigger.
   - **Application**: Trance gate and rhythmic chopping effects on pads, synths, or drum rooms. Also useful for tempo-synced stereo panning to add rhythmic movement to static elements in a mix.

**[MeldaProduction MTremolo](https://www.meldaproduction.com/MTremolo)** (Free — part of MFreeFXBundle)
   - **Function**: Tremolo with fully editable waveform shapes and harmonics. Automatically syncs to host tempo.
   - **Application**: Rhythmic volume modulation with more waveform control than a standard tremolo. Use when a simple sine/square tremolo isn't enough — the editable waveform lets you dial in exactly the pulse shape you want.
